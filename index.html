CHAPTER ONE

Once upon a time,
there was us.

I don’t think either of us actually knows when our friendship started.

Like, there was no specific day where we woke up and decided, “Chalo Muzhdah aaj se hum dost hain.” 😭

It just happened.

And the funniest part is that the whole thing started because of Ma’am Aisha’s paper, the lady I hate💀

Still, somehow, that horrible paper brought me to you.

And if someone had told me at that time that one day I would be sitting here writing an entire chapter about our friendship, I would’ve probably said, “Pagal ho? Hum dono? Abyyy chall” 😂

Because we were SO different.

I was an extrovert. And not just an extrovert, but the kind who could literally start talking to a stranger and somehow know their entire life story within ten minutes. 😭

And you?

You were the complete opposite.

Quiet. Introverted. Reserved. Very much in your own little world.

And I remember thinking that you probably wouldn’t like me.

I genuinely thought you’d judge me HARDDDDD!!!

I was like, “Muzhdah is probably going to think I’m too loud, too social, too much.” 😭

And honestly, you did avoid me a little in the beginning.

But now I understand that it wasn’t because you didn’t like me.

You were just being Muzhdah.

And I was being… well… ab apni kya hi tareef krun. 😂

Somehow, despite the fact that our vibes seemed completely different, life kept putting us in the same places.

Until one day, we walked back from the department to the hostel together.

And that walk was probably one of the most normal things we’ve ever done.

But I don’t know why, when I think about the beginning of us, I always come back to that walk.

Because that’s when we actually started talking.

Not the “haan paper ka kya karna hai” type of talking.

We actually started getting to know each other.

We talked, we walked, we slowly became comfortable with each other.

And somewhere between the department and the hostel, the girl I thought might judge me started becoming someone I genuinely wanted to know better.

Then came the first time I went to your room.

Well… common room, technically. 😂

Again, nothing dramatic happened.

I didn’t know that I was sitting there with someone who would eventually become such an important part of my life.

I just went to see you.

Phir main apne hostel wapas aa gayi.

And slowly, we started talking on text.

At first, obviously, it was all very academic. 🤓

“Yeh concept samajh aya?”

“Ma’am Aisha ke paper ki preparation kaise karni hai?”

“Group study kren?”

And you replied “Han krte hain”

And we ghosted each other 🤣

Very innocent.

Very educational.

Very professional.

😂

And then I told you that we should study Ma’am Aisha’s subject together because that paper was genuinely a nightmare.

I had this whole senior-girl responsibility thing going on in my head.

Because I had already had a bad experience with my own seniors. When I needed guidance, I didn’t really get it. And I knew how frustrating it was to struggle with something and have nobody properly guide you.

So I thought, “Nahi. Mere juniors mere jaisi situation face nahi karenge.”

I would teach you.

I would guide you.

I would explain things to you.

I would make sure you didn’t feel as lost as I had felt.

Very generous of me, I know. 😌😂

Although, let’s be honest, mere dimaag ke kisi kone mein ek aur reason bhi tha.

I thought that if I taught you, meri apni preparation bhi strong ho jayegi. 🤡

So technically, I was helping you while secretly helping myself too.

Smart girl. 😎😎

But then something happened that neither of us noticed.

We started with Ma’am Aisha’s paper…

and somehow we ended up preparing for Dr. Tahir’s exam together.

Then Dr. Tahir became Dr. Munawwar.

Dr. Munawwar became Dr. Noman.

And I don’t even know kab hum ek paper se poori university ke papers ke study partners ban gaye. 😭

Like seriously…

Ma’am Aisha se shuru kiya tha aur poora department cover kar diya. 💀

But it wasn’t just studying anymore.

We started going to the department together.

Department se hostel saath wapas aana.

Hostel se department saath jaana.

Shuttles mein saath khwaar hona. 😭

Waiting for each other.

Complaining about things.

Talking about random things.

Studying.

Getting tired.

Laughing.

And then doing the exact same thing the next day.

And somewhere in all those ordinary days, you became part of my routine.

I didn’t consciously decide that.

I didn’t wake up one morning and think, “Muzhdah is now an important person in my life.”

It just happened so quietly that I didn’t even notice.

There were little things too.

Like my random habit of bringing you flowers. 🌸

I don’t even remember when that started.

Kabhi koi proper reason nahi hota tha.

I’d just see flowers and think,

“Yeh Muzhdah ko de deti hoon.”

And then I’d bring them to you.

And you’d laugh.

And I don’t think you ever realized how much I loved seeing you laugh.

Especially because, in the beginning, getting that laugh out of you felt like a whole achievement. 😭😂

But slowly, something changed.

You started opening up.

You started relying on me.

You started trusting me.

And I noticed.

I noticed every single little thing.

Every time you came to me for something.

Every time you trusted me with something.

Every time you let me help you.

Every time you shared something with me that you could have kept to yourself.

Maybe you didn’t think much of those moments.

But I did.

Because I knew where we had started.

You were the girl I thought would probably judge me.

And now you were trusting me.

That meant something to me.

And then, slowly, I started doing the same.

I started sharing my own things with you.

My thoughts.

My problems.

My stupid stories.

My random frustrations.

Things I wouldn’t necessarily tell everyone.

And without realizing it, the trust stopped being one-sided.

It became ours.

You trusted me.

I trusted you.

And somewhere in between all those conversations, we stopped being two girls who were just studying together.

We became friends.

Real friends.

The kind where you don’t even remember exactly when it happened.

And honestly, I think that’s what makes our beginning so beautiful.

There wasn’t one big moment.

There were hundreds of tiny ones.

A walk.

A room.

A text.

A difficult paper.

An exam.

A shuttle ride.

A flower.

Your laugh.

My endless talking. 😭

Your slowly growing trust.

My slowly growing trust.

And so many ordinary days that didn’t feel extraordinary while we were living them.

But now?

Now I look back at those days and think…

How did we not know?

How did we not know that these stupid little moments were becoming memories we’d one day miss?

How did we not know that we were slowly creating something that would matter so much?

We thought we were just trying to survive university.

We thought we were just preparing for the next exam.

We thought we were just walking back to the hostel.

But actually…

we were becoming us.

And maybe that’s my favorite thing about our story.

It wasn’t planned.

It wasn’t forced.

It wasn’t even expected.

It was just two completely different people who somehow kept ending up beside each other.

Again.

And again.

And again.

Until being beside each other didn’t feel accidental anymore.

It just felt normal.

And then, somewhere along the way, normal became special.

And special became something I couldn’t imagine my university life without.

If I could go back and meet that version of myself, the girl who was walking back from the department with you and had absolutely no idea what was happening, I think I’d tell her one thing.

I’d tell her:

“You don’t know it yet, but this girl is going to become one of your people.”

And maybe I’d tell her to pay a little more attention.

To remember that walk.

To remember those conversations.

To remember the first flowers.

To remember the first time Muzhdah really trusted her.

Because one day, all those little things would become precious.

And now here I am, looking back at the beginning of us and realizing that our story didn’t begin with some grand moment.

It began with Ma’am Aisha’s cursed paper. 😭

Then a walk.

Then a conversation.

Then another.

Then exams.

Then flowers.

Then laughter.

Then trust.

And before we knew it…

there was us.

Once upon a time, there was us. 🌸

And honestly, Muzhdah…

I think that’s one of the most beautiful accidents that ever happened to me. ❤️
